Our story

WEBSITE NAME started in a small workshop in Lviv, Ukraine, where our founders got tired of clothes that looked good for one season and fell apart the next.

What began as a handful of hand-cut hoodies sold to friends has grown into a small catalog of everyday pieces, still made the same way: better materials, simple construction, and nothing you'll need to replace in a year.

We're a small team, we ship in small batches, and we're just getting started.
